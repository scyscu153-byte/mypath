# CareerBridge MJC Design Direction

## 1. Product definition
CareerBridge MJC is a growth navigator that connects opportunities across Myongji College to each student’s target role. It should not feel like a generic AI tool or an administrative school portal. The experience should feel official, student-friendly, practical, and trustworthy.

**Core promise:** 명전의 기회를, 내 목표에 연결하다.

## 2. Brand concept — CareerBridge MJC's "growth bridge" motif

### Visual metaphor
- **Tree / leaf:** capabilities and experiences accumulated by a student
- **Bridge / connection lines:** links between school opportunities, skills, and a career goal
- **Nodes:** current state, opportunities, skills, and target destination
- **Blue:** official information, trust, and forward movement

### Product journey
1. Current student profile
2. Target role or company
3. Required skills
4. MJC programs and activities
5. Growth record and next opportunity

## 3. Tone of voice

### Recommended
- Supportive and practical
- Clear without exaggeration
- Student-centered and approachable
- Evidence-led and action-focused

### Avoid
- “AI predicts your future” claims
- Hiring or acceptance guarantees
- Overly formal administrative language
- Cold neon-tech dashboard language

### Copy examples
- “목표에 필요한 다음 단계를 찾아드려요.”
- “현재 목표와 연결성이 높은 프로그램입니다.”
- “신청 자격은 원문에서 확인해 주세요.”
- “공식 원문과 모집 상태를 함께 확인했습니다.”

## 4. Color system

| Role | Color | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MJC Navy | #071D49 | Official identity, primary headings, dark surfaces |
| MJC Blue | #0066B3 | Main actions, verified information, focused states |
| Sky Surface | #EAF4FF | Information panels and trusted-source surfaces |
| Growth Green | #43A66B | Completed growth, active recruitment, acquired skills |
| Warning Yellow | #F2C94C | Verification-needed or schedule-check states |
| Main Text | #172033 | Primary copy |
| Secondary Text | #667085 | Supporting copy |
| Surface | #F7F9FC | Page background |
| Line | #E4E9F0 | Borders and dividers |

### Usage principle
Use navy for institutional confidence and blue for action. Reserve green for positive growth and recruiting availability. Keep yellow limited to uncertainty and required confirmation.

## 5. Typography

### Recommended family
- IBM Plex Sans KR

### Hierarchy
- Display heading: 38px, bold, navy or white
- Section heading: 22–24px, bold
- Body: 14–15px, regular, comfortable line-height
- Labels and badges: 12–13px, semibold or bold

Typography should be concise, highly legible, and grounded in everyday student language.

## 6. Core screen structure

### Header
- CareerBridge MJC lockup
- Short proposition: “명전의 기회를, 내 목표에 연결하다”
- Student profile chip

### Opening panel
- Dark navy surface
- Small "CareerBridge MJC" wordmark label
- Target role entry
- Primary action: “내 성장 경로 분석하기”

### Growth bridge
- Current student state
- Skills to build
- Career goal
- Blue connector lines and circular nodes
- Use a green accent only for achieved or connected progress

### Verified opportunity card
Priority order:
1. Program name
2. Why it connects to the student’s goal
3. Skills gained
4. Recruiting status
5. Official-source verification
6. Source action

## 7. Trust marker system

| Status | UI language | Visual treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official source confirmed | 공식 원문 확인됨 | Blue check + sky pill |
| Source needs review | 원문 확인 필요 | Yellow alert + soft yellow pill |
| Recruiting now | 현재 모집 중 | Green check or dot + green pill |
| Upcoming | 모집 예정 | Blue dot + blue pill |
| Schedule needs review | 일정 확인 필요 | Yellow dot + yellow pill |
| Always available | 상시 | Neutral gray pill |

Trust markers are not decorative. They are a core part of the product’s differentiation and should remain visually consistent in every program card.

## 8. Interaction direction
- In analysis states, complete one growth node or leaf at a time.
- On saving a program, add a subtle leaf-growth motion.
- As users progress, fill bridge lines gradually.
- Keep motion brief, calm, and purposeful; do not make the experience feel game-like or overloaded.

## 9. Design principles
1. Show the pathway, not only a list of programs.
2. Explain why every program is recommended.
3. Separate brand colors from operational status colors.
4. Prioritize official-source confidence over AI spectacle.
5. Make school opportunities feel like practical steps toward a student’s goal.

## 10. One-sentence design summary
A Myongji College growth map that uses official blue, branching bridge connections, and verified opportunities to guide students from their current state to their target career.
